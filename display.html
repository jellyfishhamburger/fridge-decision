How this maps to hardware

  1. Screens and order are user settings (Settings → Screens & order), stored on the device. Untick a screen and the panel stops showing it; the rotation interval is a setting too.
  2. Three buttons, fixed: next screen · “I'll cook this” · “not this one”. The last two write to a log — that is the cheapest evidence in the project: does anyone actually take the suggestion.
  3. Pictograms, not illustrations. One line drawing per food (21 foods, 16 drawings, several share one). A bespoke drawing per dish would be ~18 more illustrations for no extra decision value; a dish shows the drawings of its two ingredients instead.
  4. Same database as the app. data.js holds shelf lives and dishes; both surfaces read it, so the panel can never suggest something the app does not know.
