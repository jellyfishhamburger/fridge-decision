What I changed from the sketch

  1. Order flipped. The decision goes first, the countdown second. Your sheet put “green — 3 days” first; that is information, and the research says people do not lack information, they lack the decision.
  2. Two lines of type, that is all. At one metre, 296 × 128 holds about two lines of 22 px. Every extra line is a line nobody reads from across the kitchen.
  3. Colour is a signal. Three-colour e-paper gives exactly one accent, so red is reserved for “use today / 1 day” and used nowhere else. Your yellow highlight became red plus a bigger numeral.
  4. Detail stays on the phone. Every screen ends with a one-line pointer (details & recipes → app) — that is the job the panel cannot do.
  5. One rule table for both surfaces. The panel and the app both read logic.js and the same stored items, so the screen can never recommend something the app does not know about.
